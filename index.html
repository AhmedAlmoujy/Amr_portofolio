05 — Markets

Same product. Different buyer behaviour.

A profitable campaign is never a copy-paste job across borders. Each market operates on its own payment gateways, platform ecosystems, and cultural conversation styles.

Saudi Arabia — Core Focus & Ecosystem Mastery

The primary market where I manage high-volume ad budgets. Understanding the Saudi consumer requires knowing that purchasing happens on local rails: Salla and Zid platforms, Mada debit cards, Apple Pay, and split-payment solutions (Tabby / Tamara).

Key Regional Nuance:

Ad copy written in authentic Gulf & Saudi dialect converts significantly higher than formal Modern Standard Arabic. Understanding local events like Foundation Day and National Day unlocks massive seasonal spikes.

07 — My Approach

A disciplined system, not guesswork

Every dollar allocated follows a verified sequence. Nothing scales without proof, and no campaign is evaluated without deep attribution.

01 / PHASE

Understand

I begin by diving into the business fundamentals: product margins, average order value (AOV), ideal customer profile, competitor positioning, and past ad account history.

02 / PHASE

Plan

I build the campaign blueprint: account structure, audience segmentation, creative angle matrix, budget distribution, and event tracking verification.

03 / PHASE

Launch

I deploy the campaign assets with tight quality control across copy, UTM tags, destination URLs, tracking pixels, and bid pacing limits.

04 / PHASE

Analyze

I scrutinize the early data signals: hook rates, hold rates, outbound CTR, CPM trends, cart-to-purchase ratios, and CPA variations across angles.

05 / PHASE

Optimize

I cut losing ads swiftly, iterate on winning creative hooks, rebalance budgets toward high-ROAS segments, and refine retargeting frequencies.

06 / PHASE

Scale

When the data demonstrates consistent profitability, I scale budgets vertically and horizontally while preserving target acquisition costs.
